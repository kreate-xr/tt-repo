About us

SportyXR Table Tennis. Last updated 25 October 2026.

Our mission: democratize sports excellence with XR + AI

SportyXR is a pioneering sports technology brand from India, transforming how athletes train by combining virtual reality (VR) and artificial intelligence (AI).

Our mission is to democratize access to world-class sports coaching, making it available to every aspiring athlete regardless of location or resources. We envision a future where Olympic-level training can happen anywhere, even from the comfort of your own home.

Founded by a passionate team of sports enthusiasts, engineers and designers, SportyXR started with 10m air pistol shooting. SportyXR Table Tennis brings the same approach to table tennis.

SportyXR Table Tennis

SportyXR Table Tennis is a table tennis game for Meta Quest headsets that runs in the browser at tt.sportyxr.com, with nothing to install. Play in your own room in mixed reality or in a virtual venue, against AI opponents, in Training Sessions, or against a friend in live multiplayer. The free version has no time limit; the full game is a one-time purchase. See Pricing.
