Cancellation & Refund Policy

SportyXR Table Tennis. Last updated 24 October 2026.

This policy covers the full-game unlock for SportyXR Table Tennis, sold by KREATEXR TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED at tt.sportyxr.com: a one-time payment of ₹1,777 (INR) or US$19.97 (USD) for lifetime access.

Try before you buy

Every player gets 10 minutes of free play first. Please use it to check the game runs well on your headset and that you enjoy it before you pay.

Cancellation

  • The full game is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. There is no recurring charge, so there is nothing to cancel after you have paid.
  • You can cancel a purchase at any point before the payment is completed by closing the checkout window. You will not be charged.
  • To cancel a completed purchase, ask for a refund as described below.

Refunds

  • You can ask for a full refund within 3 days of your purchase, for any reason.
  • After 3 days, purchases are non-refundable, except in the cases below.
  • If you were charged more than once for the same purchase, or you paid and the full game was not unlocked on your account, we will refund the extra or failed payment in full, whenever you tell us.
  • Once a refund is issued, the full-game unlock is removed from your account.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@sportyxr.com from the email address on your account, with the date of purchase and, if you have it, the Razorpay payment ID. We reply within 3 business days.

When you get your money back

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or wallet) and reach you within 7–14 business days of approval, depending on your bank. We do not refund to a different account or method.
