Cancellation & Refund Policy

SportyXR Table Tennis. Last updated 19 November 2026.

This policy covers the full-game unlock for SportyXR Table Tennis, sold by KREATEXR TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED at tt.sportyxr.com: a one-time payment of ₹1,997 (INR, including GST) or US$19.97 (USD) for lifetime access.

Try before you buy

The free version has no time limit. Please use it to check the game runs well on your headset and that you enjoy it before you pay.

Cancellation

  • The full game is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. There is no recurring charge, so there is nothing to cancel after you have paid.
  • You can cancel a purchase at any point before the payment is completed by closing the checkout window. You will not be charged.
  • To cancel a completed purchase, ask for a refund as described below.

Refunds

You can cancel your purchase and get a refund, for any reason, when all three of these are true:

  • it is within 3 days (72 hours) of your payment;
  • you have played the full game for less than 2 hours since buying it, as counted by the game; and
  • your account has not had a refund before. Each account can get one refund. If you buy the full game again after a refund, that new purchase cannot be refunded.
  • Outside these conditions, purchases cannot be cancelled or refunded, except in the case below or where the law requires it.
  • If you were charged more than once for the same purchase, or you paid and the full game was not unlocked on your account, we refund the extra or failed payment whenever you tell us. Duplicate payments are normally refunded automatically. These refunds do not count as your account's one refund.
  • A refund covers the whole purchase. Partial refunds are not supported.
  • Once a refund is issued, the full-game unlock is removed from your account.

How much you get back

Every refund, including one for a duplicate or failed payment, is the amount you paid, less the payment processing charges (including GST) that our payment gateway charged on your payment. These charges depend on your payment method and currency and are typically around 2–3% of the amount paid. If your payment method has no processing charge, you get the full amount back. When you request a refund in the game, it shows the exact amount refunded and the processing charges deducted, and your account screen keeps showing them afterwards. For a refund arranged by email, we send you the same figures in our reply.

How play time is counted

While your purchase can still be refunded, the game counts your play time on our servers, so it needs an internet connection to keep playing. If the game cannot reach our servers for more than about 2 minutes of play during that time, it ends the session; you can start playing again once the connection is back. This check stops for good once the 3 days have passed, you reach 2 hours of play, or your account has used its refund.

How to ask for a refund

  • In the game: sign in, open your account, and press Request a refund. The button is shown only while your purchase qualifies; otherwise the account screen tells you why it does not.
  • By email: if you cannot use the button, or for a duplicate or failed payment, email hello@sportyxr.com from the email address on your account, with the date of purchase and, if you have it, the Razorpay payment ID. We reply within 3 business days.

Buyers in the EU, UK and similar regions

Consumer law in some countries gives a 14-day right to withdraw from an online purchase. For digital content that right ends once delivery starts, if you agree to that. The full game unlocks the moment your payment succeeds, so at checkout we ask you to agree that the unlock starts immediately and that you lose the 14-day withdrawal right when it does. This policy's refunds still apply on top of that.

When you get your money back

Refunds go back to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or wallet), in the currency you paid in, and are usually credited within 5–7 working days. Payments made with international cards may take longer, depending on your card issuer. We do not refund to a different account or method.

If you paid in a currency other than your card's currency, the amount credited may differ slightly because of exchange rates applied by your bank or card issuer.

Payment disputes

If you have a problem with a payment, please contact us first at hello@sportyxr.com so we can resolve it quickly. Refunds cannot be issued while a payment dispute (chargeback) is open with your bank.
