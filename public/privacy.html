Privacy notice

SportyXR Table Tennis — beta. Last updated 6 September 2026.

This is a beta. We collect the minimum needed to find out what is broken and to tell you when the full version is ready. This page says exactly what that is, in plain terms.

Who we are

SportyXR Table Tennis is operated by the developer of this site. For any privacy question, including a request to delete your data, contact suthar.bhuvnesh@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • An anonymous ID. When you play, your browser is issued a random identifier. It is not linked to your name or your Meta account, and we cannot use it to identify you.
  • Session and gameplay data. Once per play session: when it started and ended, which mode you chose, how far you got (loaded, started playing, finished a match), match statistics such as shots and accuracy, your browser's user-agent string and screen size, network quality figures for multiplayer, and any JavaScript errors the app hit.
  • Feedback you choose to send. A rating and, if you write one, your note.
  • Your email address — only if you ask to join the early-access list, either by typing it or by signing in with Google. If you use Google, we receive your email address from Google; we do not receive your password.

What we do not collect

  • No camera or passthrough imagery ever leaves your headset.
  • No microphone audio, room scan, or spatial map of your home.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking, and no third-party analytics.

Why we collect it

Session and error data exist so we can fix faults we cannot reproduce — a headset failing to connect on a network we have never seen, for example. Feedback tells us what to prioritise. Your email is used for one thing: to tell you when the full version is available. We do not sell it, and we do not pass it to anyone for their own marketing.

Where it is stored

In Google Cloud Firestore and Google Identity Platform, operated by Google on our behalf. Session and feedback records are not readable from the app by anyone, including you — only by us, through an administrative interface.

How long we keep it

Session records are deleted automatically after 90 days. Feedback is kept for as long as the beta is useful to us. Email addresses are kept until you ask us to remove yours, or until the launch mail has gone out and you have not opted to stay on the list.

Your choices

  • You can play without ever giving an email address. Nothing is gated.
  • You can ask us to delete everything associated with you. If you signed in with Google, email us from that address and we can find your records; if you did not, tell us roughly when you played and we will do our best, but an anonymous ID may not be traceable back to you — which is the point of it.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for this domain discards the anonymous ID on your device. Records already sent remain, unless you ask us to delete them.

Children

This beta is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top. The beta is moving quickly, so it is worth re-reading if you come back after a long gap.
