Privacy notice

SportyXR Table Tennis. Last updated 9 November 2026.

We collect the minimum needed to find out what is broken and to keep the game running for you. This page says exactly what that is, in plain terms.

Who we are

SportyXR Table Tennis is operated by KREATEXR TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED (SportyXR). For any privacy question, including a request to delete your data, contact hello@sportyxr.com.

What we collect

  • An anonymous ID. When you play, your browser is issued a random identifier. It is not linked to your name or your Meta account, and we cannot use it to identify you.
  • Your account — only if you create one. Your email address and a password. The password is handled by Google Identity Platform; we never see or store it. We use the email address to sign you in, to verify it, and to send a password-reset link when you ask for one.
  • Purchase details — only if you buy the lifetime unlock. Your name and phone number (asked for on the payment screen and nowhere else), your account email, the price and currency, the order and payment references, and whether the payment succeeded or was refunded. While your purchase can still be refunded, we also count how much you play, because refunds are only available under 2 hours of play after buying. Play on the free version is not counted. Your name, email and phone are passed to Razorpay, which processes the payment. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Session and gameplay data. Once per play session: when it started and ended, which mode you chose, how far you got (loaded, started playing, finished a match), match statistics such as shots and accuracy, your browser's user-agent string and screen size, network quality figures for multiplayer, and any JavaScript errors the app hit.
  • Feedback you choose to send. A rating and, if you write one, your note.
  • Web analytics. We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits to this site and see which pages people open. It stores an identifier in your browser and records the usual web measurements — page addresses, referring site, approximate location from your IP address (we do not see the address itself), device and browser type. It is used to measure the site, not to build an advertising profile of you. Advertising and ad-personalisation signals are switched off everywhere, for everyone.
  • An early-access email — only if you type one into the early-access box. We store the address you type, nothing more. It is kept separately from your account email.
  • Abuse protection. This site is protected by Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise, which checks that requests come from a real browser and not a script. It runs invisibly and is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What we do not collect

  • No camera or passthrough imagery ever leaves your headset.
  • No microphone audio, room scan, or spatial map of your home.
  • No precise location. We never ask for GPS or device location; the only geography we see is the country/region Google Analytics infers from an IP address.
  • No advertising, ad profiling, or cross-site tracking.

Why we collect it

Session and error data exist so we can fix faults we cannot reproduce — a headset failing to connect on a network we have never seen, for example. Feedback tells us what to prioritise. Your account and purchase details exist so you can sign in, so we can take payment and keep your unlock working on any device, to decide whether a purchase can be refunded, and to keep the records that tax and accounting law requires. An early-access email is used for one thing: to tell you when the full version is available. We do not sell any of it, and we do not pass it to anyone for their own marketing.

Where it is stored

In Google Cloud Firestore and Google Identity Platform (which issues the anonymous ID), operated by Google on our behalf, and — for the web analytics above — in Google Analytics. Payments are processed by Razorpay. Session, feedback, order and play-time records are not readable from the app by anyone, including you — only by us, through an administrative interface.

How long we keep it

Session records are deleted automatically after 90 days. The network codes the earlier free trial used are no longer collected, and we delete the ones we held. Feedback is kept for as long as it is useful in improving the game. Email addresses are kept until the launch mail has gone out, or until you ask us to remove yours, whichever comes first. Your account is kept until you ask us to delete it. Purchase records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after an account is deleted.

Your choices

  • You can play the free version without giving an email address. An account is needed only to buy the lifetime unlock, and a phone number only at checkout.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland analytics storage is switched off by default — no cookie or identifier is written unless and until you opt in, and Google Analytics receives only cookieless, aggregated pings. Advertising signals are denied everywhere, for everyone, regardless of region.
  • Google Analytics also honours your browser's or headset's ad-blocking and tracking-protection settings; blocking it does not affect the game.
  • You can ask us to delete everything associated with you. If you have an account or joined the early-access list, email us from that address and we can find your records. Deleting your account also removes the lifetime unlock bought on it. If you never gave an email, tell us roughly when you played and we will do our best, but an anonymous ID may not be traceable back to you — which is the point of it.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for this domain discards the anonymous ID on your device and signs you out; you can sign back in to an account at any time. Records already sent remain, unless you ask us to delete them.

Children

SportyXR Table Tennis is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top, so it is worth re-reading if you come back after a long gap.
