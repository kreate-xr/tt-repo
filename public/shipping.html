Shipping & Delivery Policy

SportyXR Table Tennis. Last updated 19 November 2026.

No physical shipping

SportyXR Table Tennis is a digital product played online at tt.sportyxr.com. Nothing is shipped, and no shipping charges apply. We do not sell VR headsets, controllers or any other hardware.

How the full game is delivered

  • The full-game unlock is delivered to the account you are signed in with when you pay.
  • It is normally active within a few seconds of a successful payment; the game shows "Full game unlocked" when it is.
  • In rare cases, such as a delayed confirmation from your bank, it can take up to 24 hours.
  • Sign in with the same account on any headset or browser to play. There is nothing to download or install.

If the unlock does not arrive

If you have paid and the game still is not unlocked after 24 hours, email hello@sportyxr.com from your account's email address and, if you have it, the Razorpay payment ID. We will unlock it, or refund you under our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Where we sell

The game is available worldwide wherever a WebXR-capable browser can reach tt.sportyxr.com. Payments are in INR for Indian phone numbers and in USD everywhere else.
